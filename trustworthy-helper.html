How CyburrHunee makes money

            
Plain words, no checkout. We are a tech and music company: artists get paid, AI helps people instead of replacing them, and a human is in the loop every time.

            
No big-company AI. CyburrHunee does not use xAI, Grok, OpenAI, or Google AI. We use small, local, open models, artists get paid, humans stay in the loop.

            
1. Training as a service, not data as a product. A person or business pays us to train a model on their own data, or on work that is licensed and opted into. They pay for the compute, the training work, and the model they get back. Their data stays theirs. We never sell it, share it, or resell it to anyone.

            
2. Opt-in artist licensing. Songs, presets and code snippets are only used if the owner turns on a “Learn from…” switch. Those switches are OFF unless you turn them on. If opted-in work is used in a paid training run, we record the username, item and date so a cut can be calculated later. Opt out any time and the work is never used or sold.

            
3. Pay for the model, not for other people's data. Planned charges are custom training for an artist or business, a trained model download or API access to that model, and a subscription for pro training features. We do not sell access to someone else's data, and there is no dataset for sale.

            
No data sold. No ads. No tracking. The royalty record below is not tracking for ads; it is a note for possible artist payment. No payment is taken here yet — no Stripe, PayPal, checkout or sale.
